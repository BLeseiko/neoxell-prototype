Questions

Before youask

Most clients achieve optimal results with 6 sessions spaced 6–8 weeks apart. Hair grows in cycles — the laser only works in the active growth phase. Spacing sessions captures each cycle. A free follow-up check is included after your course.
Most clients describe the sensation as a warm snap — noticeably less than waxing. Our MeDioStar diode laser includes built-in contact cooling that reduces discomfort throughout the treatment.
Our diode laser is effective across Fitzpatrick skin types I–VI. It works best on dark, coarse hair. Very light blonde, red, grey, or white hair contains less melanin and may not respond as well — we assess your skin and hair type at the free intake.
Your intake includes a full skin and hair type assessment, a test patch, a personalised treatment plan, and a written price quote. There is no obligation to proceed and no deposit is required.
Duration depends on the zone. Underarms take around 15 minutes; full legs up to 60 minutes. You will receive a time estimate for your specific zones at intake.
Yes — and it is more efficient. We treat multiple zones in a single appointment. Combining 3 or more zones also qualifies for our multi-zone discount of 15%.
